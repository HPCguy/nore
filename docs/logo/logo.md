## The Symbol

The **|~|** symbol, representing the capital letter N, combines **~** (NOT) and **|** (OR), forming the NOR operation that gives the language its name. NOR is a [universal gate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NOR_gate#Functional_completeness), meaning any logical function can be built from NOR alone.

## The Color

The indigo color pays tribute to the [SGI Indigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SGI_Indigo), a workstation that shaped the history of computer graphics and high-performance computing.
